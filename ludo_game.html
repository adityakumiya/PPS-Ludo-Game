🎲 Classic Ludo

Play with 2–4 players on one device

Red's turn
⚀
Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard. Then select a highlighted token to move.

How to play

• Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the track.
• Select one of your highlighted tokens to move by the dice number.
• Rolling a 6 gives another turn.
• Land on an opponent to send them home, except on safe squares.
• Reach the finish lane and roll the exact number to finish.
• First player to finish all 4 tokens wins.
